# 🌾 AGRIMART — AI-Driven Digital Agricultural Marketplace

**Connecting farmers and consumers through technology, transparency, and smarter agricultural market access.**

AGRIMART is a digital agricultural marketplace designed to connect farmers directly with consumers. The platform helps farmers list their agricultural products, manage availability, and reach potential buyers while enabling consumers to discover products, compare prices, and place orders.

The project also integrates agricultural market-price information and intelligent features intended to improve transparency and support better decision-making across the agricultural supply chain.

## 🌱 Overview

Traditional agricultural supply chains can involve multiple intermediaries, limited market visibility, and difficulties accessing reliable price information.

AGRIMART aims to address these challenges through a digital platform where farmers and consumers can interact more directly.

The application combines a web-based marketplace, backend services, database storage, agricultural market-price information, and intelligent agricultural features.

### Our Vision

To make agricultural commerce more accessible, transparent, and efficient by providing farmers and consumers with a shared digital platform.

---

## ❗ Problem Statement

Farmers may face several challenges when selling agricultural produce:

* Limited access to potential buyers.
* Dependence on intermediaries.
* Difficulty comparing agricultural market prices.
* Challenges managing product availability and orders.
* Limited visibility into delivery and order progress.

Consumers may also find it difficult to discover available agricultural products and understand their prices and origins.

AGRIMART addresses these challenges by bringing important marketplace activities into one application.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

1. Provide farmers with a digital platform to list agricultural products.
2. Help consumers discover and purchase available products.
3. Improve access to agricultural market-price information.
4. Support product, inventory, order, and delivery-status management.
5. Provide role-based access to relevant platform features.
6. Explore intelligent features such as agricultural price prediction and AI-assisted interactions.
7. Improve transparency between participants in the agricultural supply chain.

## ✨ Features

The following sections describe the project's intended capabilities. Availability of individual features depends on the current implementation and configuration.

### 🛒 Digital Agricultural Marketplace

* Agricultural product listings.
* Product search and discovery.
* Product pricing and availability information.
* Farmer-managed product details.
* Access to available products for consumers.

### 👨‍🌾 Farmer Features

* Farmer account and profile management.
* Agricultural product listing and management.
* Product price and quantity information.
* Order and logistics-status workflows, where implemented.

### 🛍️ Consumer Features

* Browse available agricultural products.
* Search for products.
* Review product information and prices.
* Access order-related functionality supported by the application.
